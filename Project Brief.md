# Project Brief
## Spatial question
Which LGAs in Abuja (FCT) have the lowest road density (km of road per km²)?
## Metric
Road density = total length of roads (km) within each LGA ÷ LGA area (km²).  
LGAs will be ranked from lowest to highest road density.
## Study area
Abuja (Federal Capital Territory), Nigeria – all six area councils (LGAs):  
Abuja Municipal, Bwari, Gwagwalada, Kuje, Kwali, Abaji.
